## WACK MUZIC 2.1: your voice, any instrument

This update rebuilds how your own voice and beatbox work. Every change is live: move a knob and hear it straight away.

### New
- **🎤 Voice FX panel on every recording:** pitch up or down (±12 semitones plus fine tune, timing kept), Auto-Tune with a Natural to Robotic strength control, reverb (room, hall, cathedral) and echo. Your original recording is never changed.
- **🤖 Voice synth:** turns your voice into an electric robot, talkbox or "choir of me" synth that sings in the song's key. Also includes Alien, Monster, Chipmunk, Telephone, Megaphone, Radio, Underwater and Lo-fi.
- **🎧 Hear myself live:** sing through the effects in real time, with a live tuner that shows your note and how far off it is.
- **🗣→🎹 Sound Mapper:** beatbox, click, pop or hum. Each different sound becomes its own group, and you choose what each group becomes: any drum, any instrument (it can follow the melody you hummed), or your own sound as a playable instrument. Your rhythm is kept exactly.
- **Quick-start bar in the studio:** Record my voice · Beatbox → beat · Sound → instrument · Create a song · Effects.
- **💾 Bake effects:** prints the effects into a new take that you can chop or turn into an instrument.
- **Living logo:** the logo and the neon sign on the home page beat like a pulse monitor, with an electric current running through them.

### Fixed
- Pitch shift no longer snaps to a note, and effects on audio-thread voices now also apply in exports.
- Sound Lab adds **Reverb · room**, **Reverb · hall** and **Echo + reverb**, and reverb tails are no longer cut off.
- "Record my voice" no longer records onto a muted track.

### Install
- **WACK-MUZIC-Setup-2.1.0.exe:** installs, or upgrades 2.0 or 1.0. Your projects and recordings are kept. If 2.0 is installed, it offers this update by itself.
- **WACK-MUZIC-Portable-2.1.0.exe:** runs without installing. It doesn't auto-update.

**First run:** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
